# Manitoba Agricultural Services Corporation (MASC)

The Manitoba Agricultural Services Corporation (MASC) is a Crown corporation of the Province of Manitoba that provides agricultural producers with comprehensive lending and risk protection solutions, including AgriInsurance, Wildlife Damage Compensation, and specialized loan products.
