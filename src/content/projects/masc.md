# Manitoba Agricultural Services Corporation (MASC)

The Manitoba Agricultural Services Corporation (MASC) is a Crown corporation of the Province of Manitoba that provides agricultural producers with comprehensive lending and risk protection solutions, including AgriInsurance, Wildlife Damage Compensation, and specialized loan products.

## Platform Highlights

- **Producer-Focused Financial Tools**: Interactive lending rate calculators, loan application guides, and custom amortization estimators designed for Manitoba farm operations.
- **Manitoba Management Plus Program (MMPP)**: Data-intensive crop yield indices, variety performance records, and historical agricultural metrics presented via responsive search tools.
- **Streamlined Service Delivery**: Consolidated digital portal architecture unifying loan application processing, tax rebates, and producer insurance accounts.
- **Accessibility & Bilingual Standards**: Engineered to satisfy public sector web accessibility (WCAG) guidelines with seamless bilingual English and French presentation.
