# Kivalliq Inuit Association (KIA)

The Kivalliq Inuit Association (KIA) represents Inuit residing throughout the Kivalliq region of Nunavut—including Arviat, Baker Lake, Chesterfield Inlet, Coral Harbour, Naujaat, Rankin Inlet, and Whale Cove. Established to defend and promote Inuit rights and interests, KIA oversees vital cultural, social, and economic development programs.

## Platform Highlights

- **Community Beneficiary Hub**: Centralized access points for Inuit enrollment, community scholarship applications, elder support grants, and economic development funding.
- **Inuit Qaujimajatuqangit Integration**: Web experience structured around traditional knowledge and values, with multilingual support embracing Inuktitut syllabics alongside English.
- **Departmental Media & Transparency**: Dynamic publishing pipeline for regional news, board elections, community meetings, and strategic resource management announcements.
- **High-Performance Responsive Design**: Optimized page delivery ensuring accessibility across Northern networks with constrained connectivity and varying device bandwidth.
