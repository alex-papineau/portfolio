# Virtual Ant Farm

A multi-agent simulation simulating colony behaviors, worker foraging cycles, obstacle navigation, and dynamic pheromone evaporation.

## Mechanics
- **Agent Sensing**: Individual ants observe sensory radiuses for food and nest direction.
- **Pheromone Trails**: Chemical gradients decay over time, demonstrating emergent swarm intelligence.
- **Obstacle Collisions**: Dynamic spatial partitioning for real-time physics.
