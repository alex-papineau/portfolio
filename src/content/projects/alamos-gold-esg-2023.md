# Alamos Gold 2023 ESG Report

The Alamos Gold 2023 Environmental, Social, and Governance (ESG) report is a purpose-built interactive reporting platform detailing operational sustainability progress across Alamos Gold's mining assets in Canada and Mexico.

## Platform Highlights

- **Interactive Metric Dashboards**: Chart.js data visualizations depicting energy consumption trends, greenhouse gas (GHG) emission intensities, and water recycling ratios.
- **Dynamic Site Breakdowns**: Dedicated interactive operational dossiers for Young-Davidson, Island Gold, and the Mulatos district.
- **Safety & Workforce Analytics**: Transparent presentation of Lost-Time Injury Frequency Rates (LTIFR), local hiring benchmarks, and indigenous community agreements.
- **Accessible Digital Publishing**: Fully responsive, lightweight web experience optimized for investor analysis, stakeholder reviews, and regulatory reporting.
