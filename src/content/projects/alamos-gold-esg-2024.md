# Alamos Gold 2024 ESG Report

Alamos Gold's 2024 Environmental, Social, and Governance (ESG) report is an interactive microsite showcasing the multinational mining company's progress across environmental stewardship, community partnerships, health and safety, and corporate governance.

## Platform Highlights

- **Dynamic Data Visualizations**: Built with Chart.js to render animated, interactive infographics tracking greenhouse gas (GHG) emission trajectories, water stewardship indices, and workforce diversity metrics.
- **Scroll-Triggered Narrative Flow**: Employs smooth AOS (Animate On Scroll) interactions that reveal multi-layered sustainability stories and annual milestone timelines.
- **Standards-Compliant Indexing**: Integrated mapping against Global Reporting Initiative (GRI), Sustainability Accounting Standards Board (SASB), and TCFD climate disclosure standards.
- **Interactive KPI Dashboards**: Drill-down capabilities allowing investors, local stakeholders, and regulatory partners to review historical trends across mining operations in Canada and Mexico.
