# International Human Rights Clinic (IHRC)

The International Human Rights Clinic (IHRC) at Robson Hall Faculty of Law (University of Manitoba) bridges the theory and practice of human rights work. The clinic provides law students with hands-on clinical legal education while delivering pro bono legal research and advocacy for individuals, communities, and non-governmental organizations.

## Platform Highlights

- **Clinical Advocacy Portfolios**: Structured dossier showcase highlighting ongoing intervention work before domestic courts, international human rights tribunals, and UN treaty bodies.
- **Academic Research & Publications**: Dedicated repository for policy briefs, shadow reports, legal guides, and student-authored working papers.
- **Client & Partner Intake Workflow**: Intuitive information architecture facilitating partnerships with grassroots organizations, civil society groups, and legal aid institutions.
- **Community-Centered Design**: Clean, accessible layout prioritizing readable legal scholarship, public interest case studies, and upcoming clinical events.
