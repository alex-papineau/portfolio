# Amazon SMT Music Companion

An integrated music companion tool designed for Amazon SMT environments to enhance audio playback workflows, streaming sync, and track analytics.

## Features
- **Real-time Media Sync**: Connects directly to playback feeds for low-latency status updates.
- **Custom Hotkeys & Overlays**: Streamlined controls without interrupting active tasks.
- **Audio Routing**: Flexible device output selection.
