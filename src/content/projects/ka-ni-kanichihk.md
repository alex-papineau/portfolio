# Ka Ni Kanichihk ("Those Who Lead")

Ka Ni Kanichihk is an Indigenous-led non-profit organization located in Winnipeg's inner city. Providing culturally grounded education, training, and social support services, Ka Ni Kanichihk delivers community-driven programming rooted in traditional First Nations and Métis values.

## Platform Highlights

- **Holistic Program Directories**: Comprehensive information pathways for specialized initiatives including the Heart Medicine Lodge, youth leadership councils, and restorative justice programs.
- **Skills Training & Employment Access**: Centralized enrollment workflows for specialized IT training, vocational mentorship, and career readiness certifications.
- **Community Events & Cultural Calendars**: Dynamic updates on seasonal ceremonies, community feasts, educational workshops, and fundraising campaigns.
- **Inclusive Information Architecture**: Designed for accessibility across community members of all ages, emphasizing welcoming visual hierarchy, plain language navigation, and mobile-first responsiveness.
