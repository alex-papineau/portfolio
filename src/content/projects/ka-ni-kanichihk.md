# Ka Ni Kanichihk ("Those Who Lead")

Ka Ni Kanichihk is an Indigenous-led non-profit organization located in Winnipeg's inner city. Providing culturally grounded education, training, and social support services, Ka Ni Kanichihk delivers community-driven programming rooted in traditional First Nations and Métis values.
